# Guided tour of the local Nexus prototype

Open the [synthetic walkthrough](http://127.0.0.1:5174). Its organization is **Nexus Walkthrough — SYNTHETIC DATA**. This is the seeded demo, separate from the ordinary [local setup](http://127.0.0.1:5173). The following route uses existing records; you can inspect it without signing a note, changing attendance, posting money or recording real audio.

| Stop | Where to go | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| 1. A clinician's day | **Overview**, in the **Clinical** workspace | Follow one existing appointment, unsigned note or task to its record. The workspace selector changes the presentation for the same organization; it does not grant another role or switch organizations. |
| 2. A client through care | **Clients** → choose an existing synthetic client → **Intake**, **Care episodes**, **Treatment plan** | Compare registration and admission evidence with the active episode/program and assigned clinician. Inspect goals and plan reviews. The chart is not a complete longitudinal medical record: coded problems, allergy completeness, medication/MAR and labs remain gaps. |
| 3. Documentation | Client's **Forms**, **Documentation**, **Knowledge & drafts**; also the main **Documentation** queue | Compare template snapshots, signed records, amendments and unsigned work. A generated proposal cites selected sources and needs explicit review; acceptance creates an *unsigned* note. A quoted passage does not prove a generated statement is clinically supported. |
| 4. Calendar and groups | **Schedule** → select an event; inspect date windows, facility time, individual visits and groups | Open the parent group or the visible child appointment. Attendance and documentation matter for billing; a scheduled visit is not evidence that care occurred. Internal recurrence and external two-way calendar sync remain future work. |
| 5. Video, recording and speech | Eligible schedule event → **Open telehealth** or **Visit records** | Inspect room/invitation and recording history where available. Recording is consented own-device media, not an automatic mixed recording. Audio processing has its own consent/review flow. Microphone/camera, multi-device relay and saved-track acceptance on this Mac are separate exercises; the tour does not simulate those results. |
| 6. Work queue | **Tasks** → inspect an existing task and its assignment, due date, priority and status | Compare it with the overview queue. Assignee visibility and completion authority can differ. Alerts, escalation, threaded messaging and a full task redesign are not demonstrated by this list. |
| 7. Biller's work | Choose **Billing** in the workspace selector → follow a queue; use **Billing & revenue** to inspect the underlying coverage, authorization, claim, payment or export | Follow a blocker back to its source. Distinguish charge, manually recorded reference, posted cash, adjustment and reversal. CSV is an internal review/export path; it is not payer submission, ERA processing or proof of a bank deposit. |
| 8. Executive's view | Choose **Executive** → inspect census/capacity, staffing, workload and financial activity | Open a drilldown and check the denominator, period and source records. Missing inputs remain visible. Cash is not profit; a readiness blocker is not a denial. This dashboard still needs customer discovery and substantial design work. |
| 9. Configuration and audit | As the local admin, **Workspace settings** → **Forms & templates**, **Facilities**, **Programs**, **Connections**, **Audit history**; inspect **Team & access** | Review the versioned program/service configuration and chronological audit. Settings depend on permissions. The connections panel distinguishes local speech capability from external integrations. |
| 10. Switch operators | Profile at **bottom left under navigation** → change appearance or choose **Switch or leave workspace** | Select another available synthetic operator and compare navigation/record access. This is local actor selection, not production sign-in. **Refresh** reloads authorized records; it does not reset either database. |

For the complete planned scope and each feature's evidence boundary, use the [50-capability ledger](acceptance-ledger.md). For the actual tested checks and what remains unverified, use the [November 4 handoff](../research/2026-11-04/final-handoff.md).
